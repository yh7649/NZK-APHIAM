# Authors and Contributors

## Author and Maintainer

**Yehyun Hong** is the project creator, primary author, and maintainer. Yehyun
is responsible for the research design, data-source selection, methodological
decisions, validation, interpretation, and release of this work.

Yehyun is a member of Princeton University's Class of 2028 in Operations
Research and Financial Engineering, with minors in Quantitative Economics,
Statistics and Machine Learning, and Neuroscience. Yehyun is a research
assistant for Net Zero Korea, a joint Princeton University-KAIST collaboration
supporting Korea's national decarbonization.

Contributors should be acknowledged from the Git history and added here when
their contribution becomes part of a release. Scholarly authorship should
follow the authorship policy of the journal or institution receiving the work.
