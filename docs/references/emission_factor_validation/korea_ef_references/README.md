# South Korea power-sector emission-factor references

Downloaded 2026-07-17.

## Direct or near-direct EF references

1. Seo, H., Kim, H., & Jeon, E. (2019). Environmental Improvement Effect and Social Benefit: Focusing on Bio-Heavy Oil Power Generation.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.7849/ksnre.2019.9.15.3.085
   - Landing page: https://journalksnre.com/_PR/view/?aidx=21026&bidx=3281
   - PDF: https://journalksnre.com/xml/21026/21026.pdf
   - File: Seo_Kim_Jeon_2019_bio_heavy_oil_EFs.pdf
   - Relevance: pollutant-specific kg/MWh values for HFO and bio-heavy-oil generation, 2015-2017; method uses monthly emissions divided by monthly generation.

2. Lee, S.-H., Lee, S. J., Moon, N., & Kim, M.-K. (2025). Impacts of the Korean Coal-to-LNG Power Plant Transition on Regional PM2.5 Concentration.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.5572/KOSAE.2025.41.6.976
   - Landing page: https://jekosae.or.kr/_EP/view/?aidx=47810&bidx=4309
   - PDF: https://jekosae.or.kr/xml/47810/47810.pdf
   - File: Lee_et_al_2025_coal_to_LNG_PM25.pdf
   - Relevance: publishes 2022 plant-complex generation and NOx/SOx/TSP mass for Korean coal plants, enabling annual kg/MWh calculation; uses CleanSYS + EPSIS.

3. Kim, N.-I. (2017). 발전부문 미세먼지 배출감소 대책의 전력시장 영향 분석. Korea Energy Economics Institute.
   - Landing page: https://www.keei.re.kr/board.es?act=view&bid=0001&cg_code=C02&list_no=81830&mid=a10101030000
   - PDF: https://www.keei.re.kr/boardDownload.es?bid=0001&list_no=81830&seq=1
   - File: KEEI_2017_power_sector_PM_reduction_market_impacts.pdf
   - Relevance: reports 2016 combined air-pollutant kg/MWh values by plant/unit group, including coal, anthracite, heavy oil, and LNG combined cycle.

4. Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy (2019). LNG는 석탄발전대비 대기오염물질 배출 감소.
   - PDF: https://www.motir.go.kr/attach/down/095a2dda9c864e1d90d751f7668a1117/794ac2b49ba21d4909dbc16f75d7a39f
   - File: MOTIE_2019_LNG_vs_coal_air_pollutants.pdf
   - Relevance: reports national 2017 coal and LNG air-pollutant kg/MWh averages, including pollutant-specific coal values.

5. Yu, J. et al. (2021). Comparison of PMtotal, PM10, PM2.5, NOx, and SO2 Emission Factors from Coal-fired Power Plants per Load Change.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.5572/ajae.2021.104
   - Landing page: https://link.springer.com/article/10.5572/ajae.2021.104
   - PDF: https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.5572/ajae.2021.104.pdf
   - File: Yu_et_al_2021_coal_EFs_by_load.pdf
   - Relevance: field-measured EFs at two 500 MW Korean pulverized-coal plants under different load conditions; factors are normalized by fuel use rather than electricity output.

6. Solutions for Our Climate. 바이오매스가 기후변화를 막을 수 있을까?
   - PDF: https://content.forourclimate.org/files/research/D4CFFUe.pdf
   - File: Solutions_for_Our_Climate_biomass_report.pdf
   - Relevance: secondary report reproducing company/National Assembly data comparing coal, LNG combined cycle, and wood-pellet emissions per generation.

## Official input-based factor manual

7. National Air Emission Inventory and Research Center (2025). 국가 대기오염물질 배출량 산정방법 편람 VII.
   - Landing page: https://www.air.go.kr/article/view.do?articleId=491&boardId=8&currentPageNo=1
   - PDF download: https://www.air.go.kr/file/download.do?fileId=699
   - File: CAPSS_Manual_VII_2025.pdf
   - Relevance: official factors by fuel and combustion technology, generally in kg per unit fuel rather than realized kg/MWh.

8. Korea District Heating Corporation. 미세먼지관리.
   - Page: https://www.kdhc.co.kr/kdhc/main/contents.do?menuNo=200363

10. Kim, J. et al. (2025). Quantification of SO2 and CO2 emission rates from coal-fired power plants in the Korean peninsula via airborne measurements.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scitotenv.2025.179430
   - Publisher page: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0048969725010678
   - Download status: publisher lists abstract-only access; no open PDF found.
   - Relevance: supporting independent validation of the SO2 emissions numerator, not directly comparable annual kg/MWh EFs.
